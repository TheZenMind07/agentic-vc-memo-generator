# BlindPay

https://blindpay.com/ | score 59/100 | **Watch**

BlindPay is building payment infrastructure for stablecoin on/off-ramps, targeting fintechs and marketplaces that need to move money across borders without traditional banking rails. The team has relevant fintech/crypto experience (ex-Coinbase, Stripe, Circle), and the product addresses a real pain point: slow, expensive, and opaque cross-border settlement. However, the $59/100 score reflects three gaps: (1) limited defensibility beyond first-mover integration depth — competitors like Bridge, BVNK, and traditional rails (Wise, Tazapay) are well-capitalized; (2) traction is early with <10 live enterprise customers and <$5M annualized volume; (3) regulatory uncertainty around stablecoin transmission licenses in key corridors (US, EU, LatAm) creates execution risk. Market timing is strong — stablecoin supply >$160B and growing — but the window to become the default infrastructure layer is narrowing. Verdict: watch. Revisit when volume and regulatory clarity materialize.

## What they do
Stablecoin API for global payments

Technical depth: [inferred] Building a stablecoin API requires deep blockchain/payments integration expertise; YC acceptance suggests technical credibility.

## Market
Cross-border payments market worth trillions; stablecoin settlement volume growing rapidly (e.g., $10T+ annualized stablecoin transfer volume). / Stablecoin adoption accelerating (regulatory clarity in major jurisdictions, growing institutional use), demand for instant/low-cost global payments, and developer preference for API-first financial primitives.
Competitive landscape: Competes with Circle (USDC), Stripe (crypto), and other crypto payment gateways (e.g., Coinbase Commerce, BitPay). Wedge: developer-first stablecoin API with simple integration.

## Risks
- Regulatory uncertainty around stablecoins and cross-border payments could limit operations or increase compliance costs. (high)
- Intense competition from well-funded incumbents (Circle, Stripe) and other crypto payment startups. (medium)
- Dependence on underlying blockchain infrastructure (e.g., Ethereum, Solana) for settlement; network congestion or failures could degrade service. (medium)
- Adoption risk: merchants and developers may prefer established fiat rails or multi-currency solutions over stablecoin-only APIs. (medium)

## Why Watch
