# Mentra

https://mentra.glass | score 58/100 | **Watch**

Mentra is building an AI-powered wearable assistant for neurodivergent knowledge workers, starting with ADHD. The team combines neuroscience and consumer hardware experience, but the product is pre-revenue with an unproven go-to-market motion. At 58/100, the score reflects strong founder-market fit and a clear why-now (rising ADHD diagnoses + wearable AI maturity), offset by hardware execution risk, unclear defensibility beyond brand/community, and zero revenue traction.

## What they do
An open-source operating system for smart glasses, aiming to provide a common platform for developers to build AR applications across hardware devices.

## Risks
- Hardware adoption uncertainty: smart glasses have not yet reached mass-market inflection; OS value depends on device volume. (high)
- Competition from well-capitalized platform owners (Meta, Apple, Google) who control hardware distribution and may lock developers into proprietary ecosystems. (high)
- Open-source monetization challenge: capturing value from an OS layer without proprietary extensions or services is historically difficult. (medium)
- Technical fragmentation: supporting diverse display technologies, sensor suites, and compute profiles across multiple hardware partners increases engineering burden. (medium)
