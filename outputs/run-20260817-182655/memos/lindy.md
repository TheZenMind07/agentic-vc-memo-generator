# Lindy

https://www.lindy.ai | score 67/100 | **Watch**

Lindy is building a no-code platform for creating AI agents that automate business workflows across apps like Gmail, Slack, Salesforce, and HubSpot. The team (ex-Google, ex-Facebook) has strong technical pedigree and early traction with 1000+ users on the waitlist and dozens of paying customers. Product demonstrates impressive agent orchestration and a growing template library. Market timing is favorable — AI agent adoption is accelerating among SMBs and mid-market. However, defensibility remains a concern: low switching costs, crowded competitive landscape (Zapier AI, Make, Relay, custom GPTs), and reliance on underlying LLM APIs. Traction is early (pre-Series A, <$1M ARR) and freshness risk is high given rapid market evolution. Score 67 reflects strong team/product/market but weak defensibility and unproven scale.
