# Foundation Alloy

https://foundationalloy.com | score 60/100 | **Watch**

Foundation Alloy is developing advanced metal alloy powders and process control software for additive manufacturing (AM), targeting aerospace, defense, and energy sectors. The team combines deep metallurgy PhDs with AM process engineering experience from national labs and prime contractors. Product centers on a proprietary alloy design platform that couples thermodynamic modeling with in-situ sensor data to qualify new materials 5-10x faster than traditional Edisonian approaches. Market entry is timed to the DoD's push for domestic supply chain resilience (Title III, AM Forward) and the qualification bottleneck for flight-critical AM parts. Early traction includes a CRADA with a national lab, two paid pilot programs with Tier 1 aerospace primes, and letters of intent for $1.2M in annual recurring revenue contingent on first-article qualification. Score of 60 reflects strong team and defensible IP, but pre-revenue status, long qualification cycles (18-36 months), and concentration risk in a handful of prime relationships keep it below the meeting threshold.
