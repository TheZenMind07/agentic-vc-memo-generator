## What they do
Robotics perception and environment understanding stack — software and algorithms that let robots see, interpret, and navigate the physical world.

## Team

Technical depth: strong

## Market
Convergence of cheaper depth sensors, matured deep learning for 3D vision, and surging demand for autonomous mobile robots in logistics/manufacturing; founders bring proprietary Face ID‑grade perception IP.
Competitive landscape: Competes with established sensor/perception platforms (e.g., NVIDIA Isaac, Intel RealSense, specialized startups like Slamcore, Zivid) and in-house robotics stacks.

- Defensibility unclear: no disclosed proprietary data moat, workflow lock‑in, or network effects; differentiation may rely solely on algorithmic superiority which can be eroded. (high)

- Market size and traction unproven: $107M funding at launch signals investor confidence but no paying customers, active usage, or product launch disclosed yet. (medium)
