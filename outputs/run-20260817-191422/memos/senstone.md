# Senstone

https://senstone.io | score 32/100 | **Pass**

Senstone scores 32/100, falling well below the threshold to even watch. The product is a smart pendant for voice notes, but the team lacks relevant hardware/software expertise, the market is niche and competitive, defensibility is low, and traction is minimal. We pass.

## What they do
Personal voice assistant for dictating ideas and notes.

## Team
Founder Nazar Fedorchuk has entrepreneurial and legal background, founded Senstone in 2016. Technical co-founder Markiyan Matsekh left in 2019, raising concerns about current technical leadership.

Technical depth: Unclear; original technical co-founder departed, no evidence of current technical leadership.

## Risks
- Technical co-founder departure may indicate instability or lack of technical depth. (medium)
- Product is a voice assistant, likely a copilot rather than an AI-native agent; may not meet thesis criteria. (high)
- No evidence of recent traction or freshness; Kickstarter/HAX backing may be old. (medium)

## Why Pass
Senstone fails on multiple thesis criteria. Team: Founders have limited experience in hardware, AI, or consumer electronics; no notable advisors. Defensibility: Low; voice-recognition API commoditizes core tech, and the pendant form factor is easily copied. Product: Requires Bluetooth tethering to phone, has latency/accuracy issues, and adds friction vs. existing smartphone voice assistants. Market: Niche use case (hands-free voice notes) has limited TAM, and strong substitutes like Apple Watch, AirPods, or Siri/Google Assistant dominate. Why-Now: No clear macro shift or inflection point; voice assistants are already ubiquitous. Traction/Freshness: Kickstarter success but minimal post-launch sales, few user reviews, and no enterprise/partnership momentum. The 32/100 score is squarely below the 50 threshold, so the verdict is 'pass'.

## What would change my mind
- Achieve 500+ active monthly users with daily engagement and positive NPS for the voice-note feature.
- Secure a strategic partnership with a major smartwatch or wearable accessory brand for distribution.
- Launch a software-only subscription model that reduces reliance on hardware sales and shows 10%+ MoM growth in paid users.
